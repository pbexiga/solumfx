Solum Builder

A circuit simulator for guitar-pedal builders that runs on your own computer. Draw a circuit, probe any point in it, turn its knobs and watch the frequency curve follow, check it against plain-English rules, and hear it through an amp model.

I built it for my own pedal work, and I'm sharing it in case it helps with yours.

The Ibanez TS808 Tube Screamer, one of the starter circuits, after a run: DC voltage at every node, and the probed point's gain, frequency curve and waveform.

Draw & probe

Click a wire, see the signal

Draw a circuit or open a starter, then click any wire or part: its waveform, harmonics and DC voltage, with gain, peaks, clipping and distortion.

Your circuits are plain files in a folder you choose. No account, no cloud.

Frequency

See what the knobs do

How loud each frequency comes out at the probed point, from 10 Hz to 30 kHz, with its peak and corners marked. It follows the knobs as you turn them.

Part influence ranks the parts by how much each one moves the curve.

Edit curve lets you drag a corner or a peak, and changes the parts to match.

Bias & headroom

Know what clips first

Is each stage biased where it should be, which one clips first, and how hard do you have to play for it to happen?

The signal path is drawn as a chain from guitar to amp, with the level at every stage for a soft, medium and hard pick.

Audio

Hear it

A guitar recording through the circuit, then through an amp model and a cab. Switch between Dry, Pedal and Snapshot while it plays.

Snapshot is a take you keep, to compare against after a change.

You bring your own recording, NAM capture and cab IR.
