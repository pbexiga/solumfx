SOLUM

/ˈsoʊ.ləm/ SOH · lum

The ground beneath our feet. The earth from which we grow. The solitude of standing alone but being complete.

Coimbra · Portugal

Solum is a one-person workshop in Coimbra, Portugal. I'm a hobbyist who likes maintaining guitars and building pedals.

I look after guitars for players in the Coimbra area. I'm designing pedals to build in small batches, with Solum Builder, the free circuit simulator I made for the job.
