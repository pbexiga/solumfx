Guitar work

Guitar work

Keeping guitars playing the way they should. Done by hand, for players in and around Coimbra.

01

Setups

Make it comfortable, make it play in tune.

  • Neck reliefTruss rod adjusted so the neck sits just right.
  • ActionString height at the bridge and the nut, set to how you play.
  • NutSlots checked and dressed so strings sit properly and open notes ring true.
  • IntonationEvery string in tune with itself all the way up the neck.
  • Restring and cleanNew strings, and a proper clean of the fretboard, hardware and body.
02

Electronics

When it crackles, cuts out, or just doesn't sound like it should.

  • PotsReplaced or cleaned, and matched to the pickups.
  • JacksA loose, noisy or dead output jack, fixed.
  • SwitchesPickup selectors and mini switches replaced or repaired.
  • RewiringA tidy, fresh harness when the old one is beyond saving.
  • ShieldingCopper or paint in the cavities to quiet hum and buzz.
  • Fault findingTracing the problem: dead pickups, hum, bad earths, intermittent signal.
  • ModsCoil split and other wiring mods, to taste.
03

Upgrades

Parts that change how a guitar plays or sounds.

  • Pickup installsFitted, wired and set to the right height.
  • TunersBetter tuning stability, fitted straight.
  • BridgesSwapping or upgrading the bridge, with the guitar set up afterwards.
  • TailpiecesStop tailpieces and wrap-arounds, fitted and adjusted.
  • NutsA new nut in bone, graphite or whatever suits.
  • HardwareKnobs, strap buttons, pickguards and the small stuff.
