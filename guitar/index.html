Guitar work

Setups, electronics and upgrades

I enjoy getting a guitar to play the way it should. This is the work I do, for anyone in or around Coimbra.

01

Setups

Make it comfortable, make it play in tune.

  • Neck reliefThe truss rod adjusted to give the neck the right amount of bow.
  • ActionString height at the bridge, set to how you play.
  • Nut slotsThe existing nut checked and its slots dressed, so strings don't bind and open notes play in tune.
  • IntonationEvery string in tune with itself all the way up the neck.
  • Restring and cleanNew strings, and a clean of the fretboard, hardware and body.
02

Electronics

When it crackles, cuts out, or just doesn't sound like it should.

  • PotsScratchy or worn pots cleaned or replaced, at the right value for the pickups.
  • JacksA loose, noisy or dead output jack, fixed.
  • SwitchesPickup selectors and mini switches replaced or repaired.
  • RewiringA new harness when the old wiring is beyond saving.
  • ShieldingCopper or paint in the cavities to quiet hum and buzz.
  • Fault findingTracing the cause of hum, bad earths, intermittent signal or a pickup that has gone quiet.
  • ModsCoil splits and other wiring changes.
03

Upgrades

Parts that change how a guitar plays or sounds.

  • PickupsNew pickups fitted, wired in and set to height.
  • TunersReplacement tuners, fitted straight and secure.
  • BridgesA replacement bridge fitted, and the guitar set up to suit it.
  • TailpiecesStop tailpieces and wrap-arounds, fitted and adjusted.
  • New nutA replacement nut, cut and shaped, in bone, graphite or another material.
  • HardwareKnobs, strap buttons, pickguards and other small parts.
